\subsection*{Glossary of Domain Terms}
\label{subsec:glossary}

Key domain terms appear below in a single authoritative glossary.

\begin{description}[style=nextline, leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-abstention}{Abstention}] Explicit outcome (\texttt{inconclusive}) where Verity withholds a directional summary because signals are weak, conflicting, missing, or out-of-domain (Section~\ref{subsec:formalization}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-assessment}{Assessment}] Structured decision-support summary of statistical, structural, or provenance signals; never a proof of factual truth or author intent (Section~\ref{subsec:context-objective}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-bounded-task}{Bounded Detection Task}] Supervised classification between defined positive and negative origin/edit classes on frozen benchmarks, excluding opinion, satire, and open-world fact-checking (Section~\ref{subsec:limitations}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-calibrated}{Calibrated Probability}] Numeric score $p \in [0, 1]$ matching empirical positive-class frequency among items assigned $p$; raw neural logits are uncalibrated (\hyperref[A3]{A3}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-coverage}{Coverage}] Proportion of eligible benchmark items receiving a directional prediction rather than abstention or error: $|A|/N$ (Section~\ref{subsec:formalization}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-ephemeral}{Ephemeral Processing}] Handling selections purely in transient memory without persistent raw storage/logs, releasing application-owned references within \MaxRetentionTime{} of terminal status (\hyperref[FR9]{FR9}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-fp}{False Positive (FP) and False Positive Rate (FPR)}] Misflagging a legitimate negative item as positive/synthetic; $\text{FPR} = \text{FP}/(\text{FP}+\text{TN})$ (Section~\ref{subsec:formalization}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-grounded}{Grounded Explanation}] Explanation where every factual assertion links to an observed signal or accessible external source, and inferences are labelled separately (\hyperref[FR7]{FR7}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-inconclusive}{Inconclusive Status}] Terminal status where analysis ran cleanly without runtime crash, but signals were weak, conflicting, or outside supported genres (\hyperref[FR4]{FR4}, \hyperref[SR1]{SR1}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-lowrisk}{Low-Risk Signal Summary (``Low-Risk Badge'')}] Single authoritative definition: a directional low-signal summary permitted \emph{only} when $\text{status}(r)=\texttt{completed}$ ($a(r) \land v(r)$, Section~\ref{subsec:formalization}) and calibrated score satisfies $\hat{p}(r) \le 0.5 - \tau_{\text{abs}}$ ($\hat{o}=0$). It is strictly forbidden when $\text{status}(r) \in \{\texttt{pending},\allowbreak\texttt{inconclusive},\allowbreak\texttt{failed},\allowbreak\texttt{cancelled}\}$ (\hyperref[INV2]{INV2}, \hyperref[FR4]{FR4}, \hyperref[SR4]{SR4}), and never guarantees authenticity (\hyperref[SR3]{SR3}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-macrof1}{Macro-$F_1$}] Unweighted arithmetic mean of per-class $F_{1,c}$ scores across classes $c \in C$ (Section~\ref{subsec:formalization}).
    \item[\hypertarget{gls-isolation}{Request Isolation}] Observable property that concurrent submissions and sessions retain unguessable independent bindings and never read or overwrite each other (\hyperref[FR10]{FR10}).
\end{description}

\subsection*{Notations and Document Conventions}
\label{subsec:conventions}

Normative requirements use \textbf{``shall''} to specify externally observable black-box behaviour (``what, not how''). Identifier prefixes partition Goals (\textbf{G1}--\textbf{G6}), Constraints (\textbf{C1}--\textbf{C6}), Assumptions (\textbf{A1}--\textbf{A5}), Invariants (\textbf{INV1}--\textbf{INV4}), Use Cases (\textbf{UC1}--\textbf{UC4}), Functional (\textbf{FR1}--\textbf{FR14}), Non-Functional (\textbf{NFR1}--\textbf{NFR10}, \textbf{NFR.E.1}--\textbf{NFR.E.5}), verbatim Hazard-linked Safety/Security (\textbf{SR1}--\textbf{SR9}), and Likely/Unlikely Changes (\textbf{LC1}--\textbf{LC6}, \textbf{ULC1}--\textbf{ULC4}). MoSCoW priorities (\textbf{M/S/C/W}) govern Section~\ref{subsec:phase-in}, and full dependency links reside in Table~\ref{TblTraceabilityMatrix}. Every diagram defines boxes, borders, colors, and arrows in its own legend.

This section defines the product problem context, differentiable value, high-level selling-point goals (\hyperref[G1]{G1}--\hyperref[G6]{G6}), behavioural overview, exclusions, stakeholder sources, and personas.

\subsection{Context and Overall Objective}\label{subsec:context-objective}

Web users routinely confront suspicious text, product reviews, and images created or altered using generative Artificial Intelligence (\AI{}) and Large Language Models (\LLM{}s). Existing ``real vs.\ fake'' checkers create two symmetric harms: accepting synthetic scams causes deception loss, while trusting uncalibrated percentage scores leads to unfair accusations against human writers—especially non-native English (\ESL{}) authors—and genuine photos missing metadata.

\textbf{Verity} provides an on-demand desktop browser extension and analysis pipeline that inspects only user-selected webpage text or static images. Rather than issuing binary truth verdicts, Verity presents source-grounded evidence, warns plainly that false flags can occur, enters \TermInconclusive{} when signals conflict or fall out-of-domain, recommends verification steps, and processes submissions ephemerally with zero default persistence.

\subsection{Current Situation and Differentiable Value}\label{subsec:current-situation}

Commercial checkers suffer from three practical gaps: (1)~\emph{opaque percentages} (e.g., ``92\% AI'') misread as fraud probabilities; (2)~\emph{copy-paste friction and data retention} onto external servers; and (3)~\emph{conflation of statistical anomalies with factual truth} (for instance, treating stripped Exchangeable Image File Format (\EXIF{}) tags as forgery). Verity differentiates itself by keeping checks inside the active page, binding every claim to an observed signal or explicit inference, separating \TermInconclusive{} and \texttt{failed} states from low-risk verdicts, and enforcing zero persistent payload storage by default.

\subsection{Product Goals (High-Level Selling Points)}\label{subsec:product-goals}

The six product goals below articulate non-expert user value; all numeric fit criteria reside in Sections~\ref{subsec:func-reqs} and \ref{subsec:nfr-reqs}.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]
    \item \phantomsection\label{G1}\textbf{G1 --- Evidence-First Assessment:} Show concrete text, image, and metadata signals behind every check instead of unexplained binary scores.
    \item \phantomsection\label{G2}\textbf{G2 --- Honest Uncertainty and Safe Failure:} Clearly surface weak, conflicting, or timed-out states via \TermInconclusive{} and \texttt{failed} outcomes plus next verification steps, preventing false reassurance.
    \item \phantomsection\label{G3}\textbf{G3 --- Defensible, Low-False-Accusation Quality:} Catch generation/manipulation signals while bounding false accusations on legitimate writing/images using reproducible benchmarks.
    \item \phantomsection\label{G4}\textbf{G4 --- Non-Expert Understandability:} Translate Machine Learning (\ML{}) and provenance evidence into plain, non-accusatory language accessible without technical training.
    \item \phantomsection\label{G5}\textbf{G5 --- Seamless In-Context Responsiveness:} Inspect selections directly on the active webpage rapidly enough to support normal browsing.
    \item \phantomsection\label{G6}\textbf{G6 --- Privacy-First Ephemeral Handling:} Read only user-selected content, gate any external transfer behind prior consent, and release transient copies within \MaxRetentionTime{}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Functionality Overview}\label{subsec:func-overview}

Verity separates two workflows: (1)~an \textbf{Interactive Browser-Extension Workflow} where a user selects visible text or a static image, receives validation against supported bounds, grants optional consent, and views an evidence-backed result card; and (2)~an \textbf{Offline Evaluation \CLI{} Workflow} used by engineers to benchmark frozen candidate detectors on held-out labelled corpora and demographic subgroups without touching live user inputs.

\subsection{Scope Limitations and Exclusions}\label{subsec:limitations}

Verity strictly enforces the following scope boundaries:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Task Scope vs.\ Truth/Opinion Exclusions (\href{https://github.com/richy1337/Verity/issues/15}{Issue \#15}, \href{https://github.com/richy1337/Verity/issues/58}{\#58}):} Verity evaluates statistical generation, manipulation, and provenance signals on a \hyperlink{gls-bounded-task}{Bounded Detection Task}. Two rules stay distinct: \emph{(a)~Task exclusion:} Verity never evaluates objective factual truth, author intent, or credibility of subjective opinion, satire, or creative fiction; \emph{(b)~Generation-domain gate:} an explicit applicability check in \hyperref[FR4]{FR4} verifies that submitted text lies within supported expository/informational domains, abstaining as \texttt{inconclusive} on opinion, satire, poetry, or out-of-domain genres.
    \item \textbf{Benchmark-Backed Claims Only:} Synthetic-origin corpora (\texttt{GenImage}/\texttt{HC3}/\texttt{RAID}; \texttt{CIFAKE} excluded from product gates due to $32\times32$ inputs) do not validate arbitrary splicing/retouching unless separately benchmarked (\hyperref[FR12]{FR12}, \hyperref[NFR1]{NFR1}). Missing a modality's Proof of Concept (\POC{}) gate restricts Revision~0 output to descriptive/metadata signals or drops that modality with \TA{} approval.
    \item \textbf{Excluded Features:} Audio/video stream deepfakes, whole-page background scraping, automated moderation takedowns, user accounts/persistent history, and training foundation models from scratch are all excluded.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Environmental Effects:} \textbf{EF1} encourages primary-source verification; \textbf{EF2} reads DOM nodes strictly read-only without mutating host pages; \textbf{EF3} bounds compute and payload size per user trigger.

\noindent\textbf{System Invariants:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]
    \item \phantomsection\label{INV1}\textbf{INV1 (Status Exclusivity):} Every admitted request $r \in \mathcal{R}$ holds exactly one state $\text{status}(r) \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{status}}$ (Section~\ref{subsec:formalization}). Invalid selections rejected at pre-admission (\hyperref[FR2]{FR2}) never enter $\mathcal{R}$.
    \item \phantomsection\label{INV2}\textbf{INV2 (No Silent Reassurance):} A request in $\{\texttt{pending},\allowbreak\texttt{inconclusive},\allowbreak\texttt{failed},\allowbreak\texttt{cancelled}\}$ never produces a \TermLowRisk{} (\hyperref[SR4]{SR4}, \hyperref[FR4]{FR4}).
    \item \phantomsection\label{INV3}\textbf{INV3 (Strict Request Isolation):} Distinct requests $r \neq q$ never expose or overwrite each other's payloads or results (\hyperref[FR10]{FR10}, \hyperref[SR7]{SR7}, \hyperref[SR9]{SR9}).
    \item \phantomsection\label{INV4}\textbf{INV4 (Zero Persistence \& Bounded Cleanup):} Raw content never enters persistent storage/logs; request work halts and application-owned transient references release within \MaxRetentionTime{} after terminal decision (\hyperref[FR9]{FR9}, \hyperref[NFR4]{NFR4}, \hyperref[SR8]{SR8}).
\end{itemize}

\textit{Mitigates false accusations (\textbf{H1}), false reassurance (\textbf{H2}), over-trust (\textbf{H5}).} &
UI/API audit on borderline, conflicting, and low-signal cases. (\hyperref[FR3]{FR3}, \hyperref[FR4]{FR4})\\
\midrule

\phantomsection\label{SR2}\textbf{SR2} & No Personal Accusation \textit{(Func.\ Safety)} &
\textbf{Statement:} The system result shall describe observed evidence and statistical signals about the submitted content item and shall not accuse its author, publisher, or depicted subject of fraud, dishonesty, or wrongdoing.\newline
\textit{Eliminates direct accusation wording (\textbf{H1}, \textbf{H5}).} &
2-reviewer semantic tone \& lexical audit across positive/negative cards. (\hyperref[FR3]{FR3}, \hyperref[FR5]{FR5})\\
\midrule

\phantomsection\label{SR3}\textbf{SR3} & No Safety Guarantee \textit{(Func.\ Safety)} &
\textbf{Statement:} The system shall not describe any submitted item as guaranteed safe, authentic, or truthful, and shall instruct the user to verify the primary source independently before acting on high-stakes content.\newline
\textit{Eliminates false guarantees (\textbf{H2}, \textbf{H5}).} &
Inspection confirming non-guarantee notice and verification guidance on all cards. (\hyperref[FR5]{FR5}, \hyperref[NFR2]{NFR2})\\
\midrule

\phantomsection\label{SR4}\textbf{SR4} & Safe Failure Handling \textit{(Func.\ Safety)} &
\textbf{Statement:} A request timeout ($\ge \MaxResponseTime{}$) or unrecoverable dependency failure shall produce a \texttt{failed} status, and partial signal output that cannot support an assessment shall produce an \texttt{inconclusive} status identifying the missing analysis; missing output shall never be mapped to evidence of low risk.\newline
\textit{Eliminates silent safe fallback (\textbf{H6}).} &
Fault/timeout injection confirms \texttt{failed}/\texttt{inconclusive} without low-risk fallback. (\hyperref[INV2]{INV2}, \hyperref[FR6]{FR6})\\
\midrule

\phantomsection\label{SR5}\textbf{SR5} & Grounded Explanations \textit{(Security/Safety)} &
\textbf{Statement:} Every displayed factual assertion about a submitted item shall have a locator to an observed input signal or accessible external source that supports that assertion; model inferences shall be explicitly labelled and shall not be presented as verified facts. Instructions embedded within submitted text or image metadata shall never override this rule.\newline
\textit{Mitigates hallucination \& prompt injection (\textbf{H3}).} &
Paired clean/injected tests \& locator resolution verification. (\hyperref[FR7]{FR7})\\
\midrule

\phantomsection\label{SR6}\textbf{SR6} & Untrusted Sandboxing \textit{(Security)} &
\textbf{Statement:} The system shall reject unsupported or malformed inputs, shall never execute active content (HTML/JavaScript/SVG scripts) inside selected items, and shall enforce per-request payload size (\MinTextLength{}--\MaxTextLength{}, $\le \MaxImageSize{}$), dimension (\MinImageDim{} to \MaxImageDim{}), and execution time ($\le \MaxResponseTime{}$) bounds.\newline
\textit{Eliminates script execution \& payload DoS (\textbf{H7}).} &
Security fuzzing with XSS, SVG scripts, and image decompression bombs. (\hyperref[FR2]{FR2}, \hyperref[NFR8]{NFR8})\\
\midrule

\phantomsection\label{SR7}\textbf{SR7} & Request Binding \textit{(Func.\ Safety)} &
\textbf{Statement:} Every displayed result shall be bound to exactly one initiating request identifier, and a late or out-of-order response from an earlier or cancelled submission shall never overwrite the result displayed for a newer submission.\newline
\textit{Mitigates stale overwrites (\textbf{H8}).} &
Out-of-order inverted-delay \& cancel-race tests over \MaxConcurrentReqs{}. (\hyperref[INV3]{INV3}, \hyperref[FR10]{FR10}, \hyperref[FR11]{FR11})\\
\midrule

\phantomsection\label{SR8}\textbf{SR8} & Ephemeral Cleanup \textit{(Privacy/Sec.)} &
\textbf{Statement:} The browser extension shall access webpage content only upon an explicit user-triggered check using least-privilege permissions, shall never collect unselected page content, browsing history, or credentials, shall require prior informed consent before any external provider transfer, shall never persist raw selected content, and shall stop request-owned processing and release application-owned transient copies within \MaxRetentionTime{} after every terminal outcome. External providers shall be disabled unless their documented retention terms are compatible with this policy; consent does not establish remote deletion.\newline
\textit{Mitigates privacy leaks (\textbf{H4}).} &
Capture audit, declined-consent packet check, zero-persistence \& release monitoring. (\hyperref[INV4]{INV4}, \hyperref[FR8]{FR8}, \hyperref[FR9]{FR9})\\
\midrule

\phantomsection\label{SR9}\textbf{SR9} & Session Isolation \textit{(Privacy/Sec.)} &
\textbf{Statement:} A user session shall not be able to retrieve, inspect, or receive another user's or concurrent session's submission or analysis result through predictable request identifiers or shared processing state.\newline
\textit{Mitigates cross-session leaks (\textbf{H9}).} &
Multi-session concurrency and foreign-ID enumeration security tests. (\hyperref[INV3]{INV3}, \hyperref[FR10]{FR10})\\
\end{tabularx}

Verity exposes four black-box external interfaces:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Extension User Interface (\GUI{}):} Context-menu trigger and an extension-owned result surface whose controls/results cannot be altered by host-page CSS or DOM manipulation (Shadow DOM styling alone does not substitute for extension isolation).     \item \textbf{Optional External Provider Client:} Outbound \HTTPS{} client gated by \hyperref[FR8]{FR8} consent, disabled without compatible retention terms, enforcing \MaxResponseTime{} deadlines and mapping HTTP/network faults strictly via \hyperref[SR4]{SR4}.
    \item \textbf{Offline Evaluation Interface (\CLI{}):} Command-line runner reading frozen dataset manifests/configs and emitting reproducible \JSON{} evaluation logs (\hyperref[FR12]{FR12}, \hyperref[FR14]{FR14}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Undesired Event Handling}\label{subsec:undesired-events}

Under abnormal events, Verity fails safely without leaking data or misleading users:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Partial Failure / Signal Conflict:} If metadata extraction succeeds while primary detection fails or conflicts, the check enters \texttt{inconclusive} and identifies the missing signal (\hyperref[SR4]{SR4}).
    \item \textbf{Timeouts \& Concurrency Burst:} Exceeding \MaxResponseTime{} forces \texttt{failed}; rapid checks enforce exact request binding (\hyperref[FR10]{FR10}); valid bursts above \MaxConcurrentReqs{} shed excess load cleanly via retryable \texttt{failed} notices without corrupting admitted work (\hyperref[NFR8]{NFR8}).
    \item \textbf{Restricted Browser Pages:} Triggering Verity on restricted \texttt{chrome://} URLs displays an access notice rather than failing silently (\hyperref[FR1]{FR1}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Detailed Usage Scenarios (Product Use Cases)}\label{subsec:detailed-use-cases}

All four core use cases specify preconditions, flows, postconditions, and traced requirements:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=4pt]
    \item \phantomsection\label{UC1}\textbf{UC1 --- Analyse a Supported Webpage Item:} User selects valid text (\MinTextLength{}--\MaxTextLength{}) or static image ($\le \MaxImageSize{}$) and triggers Verity (\hyperref[FR1]{FR1}); Verity validates input pre-admission (\hyperref[FR2]{FR2}), checks external consent if applicable (\hyperref[FR8]{FR8}), executes detection within \MaxResponseTime{}, tags evidence sources (\hyperref[FR7]{FR7}), renders a \texttt{completed} card with false-positive warning when flagged, limitations, and verification step (\hyperref[FR3]{FR3}, \hyperref[FR5]{FR5}), and releases application transient references within \MaxRetentionTime{} (\hyperref[FR9]{FR9}).
    \item \phantomsection\label{UC2}\textbf{UC2 --- Handle Weak, Conflicting, or Out-of-Domain Signals:} Valid item enters \texttt{pending}, but scores lie inside the calibration margin, required signals conflict, or the applicability gate detects subjective opinion/satire/poetry; Verity returns \texttt{inconclusive}, explains the limit/ambiguity, suggests independent verification (\hyperref[FR4]{FR4}, \hyperref[FR5]{FR5}, \hyperref[SR1]{SR1}), and completes cleanup within \MaxRetentionTime{}.
    \item \phantomsection\label{UC3}\textbf{UC3 --- Reject Invalid Input or Handle Service/Timeout Fault:} \emph{Branch A (Pre-Admission):} Out-of-bounds, SVG/HTML script, or unsupported formats are rejected within \MaxUiRenderLatency{} prior to $\mathcal{R}$ without executing scripts (\hyperref[FR2]{FR2}, \hyperref[SR6]{SR6}). \emph{Branch B (Runtime Fault):} Timeouts ($\ge \MaxResponseTime{}$) or crashes set \texttt{failed}, name missing signals without low-risk wording (\hyperref[FR6]{FR6}, \hyperref[SR4]{SR4}), and release references within \MaxRetentionTime{}.
    \item \phantomsection\label{UC4}\textbf{UC4 --- Cancel Request or Decline External Consent:} Declining consent (\hyperref[FR8]{FR8}) or clicking \texttt{Cancel} during \texttt{pending} (\hyperref[FR11]{FR11}) transitions UI to \texttt{cancelled} within \MaxCancelUiLatency{}, transmits $0\text{ B}$ externally on decline, revokes UI binding (\hyperref[SR7]{SR7}), stops tasks and releases transient references within \MaxRetentionTime{}, and drops any late responses.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Formalization of Request Lifecycle and Evaluation Metrics}\label{subsec:formalization}

This section formalizes the state machine, request invariants, applicability/conflict rules, and benchmark metrics to remove ambiguity.

\subsubsection{Formalization of Request Lifecycle, Result Binding, and Ephemeral Privacy}

Let $\mathcal{R}$ be admitted requests and $\text{SessionID}$ isolated sessions, with evidence source tags $\mathcal{E}_{\text{src}}$ and lifecycle states $\mathcal{S}_{\text{status}}$:
\begin{align*}
\mathcal{E}_{\text{src}} &= \{\texttt{observed\_signal},\;\texttt{external\_reference},\;\texttt{inference}\}, \\
\mathcal{S}_{\text{status}} &= \{\texttt{pending},\;\texttt{completed},\;\texttt{inconclusive},\;\texttt{failed},\;\texttt{cancelled}\}.
\end{align*}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Parnas Tabular Expression for Request State Transition ($\text{status}(r,t^+)$):} Admitted after validation and consent at $t_{\mathrm{init}}$ (declined/invalid items remain outside $\mathcal{R}$). On a pending request: $d(r)$ holds when $t - t_{\mathrm{init}} \ge \MaxResponseTime{}$, $c(r)$ holds on accepted cancellation, $e(r)$ holds on unrecoverable dependency error, and $a(r)$ holds once required signal work completes or fails cleanly. Let domain/applicability gate $g(r)$ be true iff the input matches a calibrated supported domain (false on opinion, satire, poetry, or unsupported genres, \href{https://github.com/richy1337/Verity/issues/58}{Issue \#58}), and conflict predicate $k(r)$ be true iff two required directional indicators yield opposite classifications. Then $v(r)$ is a total Boolean, false until $a(r)$, and true only when $g(r) \land \neg k(r) \land \neg \text{partial}(r)$ holds and (for calibrated binary tasks) margin $|\hat p(r)-0.5| \ge \tau_{\mathrm{abs}} > 0$.
    \begingroup\small
    \[
    \begin{array}{|l|l|}
    \hline
    \textbf{Disjoint condition on a pending request} & \textbf{Next status}\\
    \hline
    c(r) & \texttt{cancelled}\\
    \neg c(r)\land(d(r)\lor e(r)) & \texttt{failed}\\
    \neg c(r)\land\neg d(r)\land\neg e(r)\land\neg a(r) & \texttt{pending}\\
    \neg c(r)\land\neg d(r)\land\neg e(r)\land a(r)\land\neg v(r) & \texttt{inconclusive}\\
    \neg c(r)\land\neg d(r)\land\neg e(r)\land a(r)\land v(r) & \texttt{completed}\\
    \hline
    \end{array}
    \]
    \endgroup
    Cancellation takes precedence over deadlines/errors, and deadlines/errors take precedence over completion. Terminal states $\mathcal{S}_{\text{term}} = \{\texttt{completed},\allowbreak\texttt{inconclusive},\allowbreak\texttt{failed},\allowbreak\texttt{cancelled}\}$ are absorbing: once $r \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{term}}$, $\text{status}(r, t)$ never changes. A \TermLowRisk{} ($\hat{p}(r) \le 0.5 - \tau_{\text{abs}}, \hat{o}=0$) is allowed \emph{only} when $\text{status}(r) = \texttt{completed}$ (\hyperref[INV2]{INV2}).
    \item \textbf{Timeout Guard (\hyperref[FR6]{FR6}, \hyperref[SR4]{SR4}):} $\forall r \in \mathcal{R},\ \forall t \ge t_{\text{init}}(r) + \MaxResponseTime{} : \text{status}(r, t) \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{term}}$.
    \item \textbf{Well-Formed Result Invariant (\hyperref[FR3]{FR3}, \hyperref[FR7]{FR7}, \hyperref[SR1]{SR1}--\hyperref[SR5]{SR5}):}
    {\small
    \[
        \forall r \in \mathcal{R} : \text{status}(r) = \texttt{completed} \implies
        \begin{cases}
            \text{result}(r).\text{evidence} \neq \emptyset, \\
            \forall e \in \text{result}(r).\text{evidence}: \text{source}(e) \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{src}}, \\
            \text{result}(r).\text{uncertainty} \neq \bot \;\land\; \text{result}(r).\text{limitations} \neq \emptyset, \\
            \text{result}(r).\text{is\_guaranteed\_authentic} = \text{false}.
        \end{cases}
    \]
    }
    \item \textbf{Session Binding (\hyperref[FR10]{FR10}, \hyperref[SR7]{SR7}, \hyperref[SR9]{SR9}):} If session $s$ has active request $r_{\text{active}}(s, t) = q$ and renders $\text{displayed}(s, t) \neq \bot$, then $\text{displayed}(s, t) = \text{result}(q) \land \text{owner}(q) = s$.
    \item \textbf{Zero Persistence \& Bounded Cleanup (\hyperref[FR9]{FR9}, \hyperref[NFR4]{NFR4}, \hyperref[SR8]{SR8}):} For interactive requests ($\text{raw}(r)$ denoting submitted payload or reconstructable excerpt), persistent stores and logs hold zero raw content at all times, and application-owned live references clear within \MaxRetentionTime{}:
    \begin{align*}
        \forall r \in \mathcal{R},\ \forall t \ge t_{\text{init}}(r) &: \text{raw}(r) \notin \text{PersistentStore}(t) \cup \text{AppLogs}(t), \\
        \forall r \in \mathcal{R},\ \forall t \ge t_{\text{term}}(r) + \MaxRetentionTime{} &: \text{TempBuffers}(r, t) = \emptyset.
    \end{align*}
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Formalization of Benchmark Evaluation and Coverage Metrics}

Let $\mathcal D=\{(x_i,y_i)\}_{i=1}^N$ be the frozen product-eligible manifest over labels $C=\{0,1\}$ ($0$ authentic, $1$ synthetic), $I=\{1,\ldots,N\}$, every item receiving $\hat o_i\in\{0,1,\texttt{inconclusive},\texttt{failed}\}$ with zero dropped items, and $A=\{i\in I:\hat o_i\in C\}$.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Coverage, Abstention and Error Accounting:}
    \begin{align*}
    \text{Coverage}&=\frac{|A|}{N} \ge \MinCoverage{}, &
    \text{AbstentionRate}&=\frac{|\{i:\hat o_i=\texttt{inconclusive}\}|}{N}, \\
    \text{ErrorRate}&=\frac{|\{i:\hat o_i=\texttt{failed}\}|}{N},
    \end{align*}
    summing to $1$. Subgroup slices $J \subseteq I$ report sample counts and $\text{Coverage}(J)=|A\cap J|/|J|$; empty slices fail as undefined.
    \item \textbf{Full-Population Diagnostic Macro-$F_1$ vs.\ Selective Acceptance:} For $c\in C$,
    \begin{align*}
    \mathrm{TP}_c&=|\{i\in I:y_i=c\land\hat o_i=c\}|,\\
    \mathrm{FP}_c&=|\{i\in I:y_i\ne c\land\hat o_i=c\}|,\\
    \mathrm{FN}_c&=|\{i\in I:y_i=c\land\hat o_i\ne c\}|.
    \end{align*}
    With $0/0\triangleq0$, $F_{1,c}^{\mathrm{all}}=2\mathrm{TP}_c/(2\mathrm{TP}_c+\mathrm{FP}_c+\mathrm{FN}_c)$ and $F_1^{\mathrm{all}}=\tfrac12\sum_{c\in C}F_{1,c}^{\mathrm{all}}$ expose abstention/error penalties over $I$. Selective acceptance $F_1^{\mathrm{assessed}}$ restricts the same counts to $A$ and must satisfy $F_1^{\mathrm{assessed}}\ge\MinFOneScore{}$ alongside overall $\text{Coverage} \ge \MinCoverage{}$.
    \item \textbf{False Positives and Common-Subset Baseline Delta:} Let $N_0=|\{i\in I:y_i=0\}|$, $A_0=|\{i\in A:y_i=0\}|$, and $\mathrm{FP}=|\{i:y_i=0\land\hat o_i=1\}|$. Require $\mathrm{FPR}_{\mathrm{all}}=\mathrm{FP}/N_0 \le \MaxFPR{}$ and (when $A_0 > 0$) $\mathrm{FPR}_{\mathrm{assessed}}=\mathrm{FP}/A_0\le\MaxFPR{}$ ($A_0=0$ fails). With baseline $b$ frozen pre-test and evaluated on identical assessed set $A$, require $F_1^{\mathrm{assessed}}-F_{1,b}^{A}\ge\MinBaselineDelta{}$; failing this gate triggers scope reduction rather than post-hoc baseline weakening.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Counterexample Check:} Predicting $5$ items per class correctly and abstaining on $990$ yields $F_1^{\mathrm{assessed}} = 1.0$, yet fails on $\text{Coverage} = 0.01 < \MinCoverage{}$ and exposes $F_1^{\mathrm{all}} = 10/505 \approx 0.0198$; replacing abstentions by failures likewise fails.
