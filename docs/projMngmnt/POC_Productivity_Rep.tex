\section{Demo Plans}

Verity's central feasibility risk is whether lightweight, reproducible text and image detectors can separate synthetic or manipulated content from authentic inputs within our error budget ($\text{Macro-}F_1 \ge 0.80$, $\text{FPR} \le 10.0\%$, $\text{Coverage} \ge 70.0\%$, and $\ge +0.10$ over a declared naive baseline) without falsely accusing formal or English as a Second Language (ESL) prose or re-compressed photographs. Our Fall text and image pilots in the \href{https://github.com/richy1337/Verity/blob/main/pdfs/CDs/MLReport/main.pdf}{ML Model Report} already surfaced two concrete failure cases: a character TF--IDF model improved on a word baseline by only $+0.007$ while misflagging 5 of 27 assessed Wikipedia human answers ($18.5\%$), and a simple statistical image pilot missed every acceptance gate.

For the live POC demonstration on \textbf{Wednesday, December 2, 2026 (5:45--6:00~pm, ITB/201)} with TA Rebecca Di Filippo, we will run live code directly on a team laptop (without slides) covering:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Workflow and Uncertainty Framing (Calvin Salsali):} Walk through how Verity distinguishes \texttt{completed}, \texttt{inconclusive}, and \texttt{failed} outcomes and surfaces source-tagged evidence without accusatory language.
    \item \textbf{Reproducible ML Evaluation Pipeline (Raymond Tao):} Run and inspect the deterministic text/image experiment harness (\texttt{docs/\allowbreak{}CDs/\allowbreak{}MLReport/\allowbreak{}experiment/}) showing calibration, abstention sweeps, baseline comparison, and per-source false-positive breakdowns.
    \item \textbf{Validation, Boundary Fixtures, and Reproducibility (Jaden Moore and Richard Han):} Execute the synthetic boundary and adversarial input generator (\texttt{test/\allowbreak{}fixtures/\allowbreak{}generate.py}) and the automated SRS/HA/design specification checks (\texttt{verify\_\allowbreak{}specification.py} and \texttt{verify\_\allowbreak{}design.py}), plus discuss the POC fallback/scope decision rules for Revision~0.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent \textbf{Scope Reconciliation with the Development Plan:} Section~9.3 of the initial \href{https://github.com/richy1337/Verity/blob/main/pdfs/DevelopmentPlan/DevelopmentPlan.pdf}{Development Plan} anticipated running a local FastAPI service alongside the CLI benchmark harness during the live POC demonstration. Because the Fall ML pilot (\texttt{experiment/}) surfaced high false-positive rates on unseen Wikipedia text (18.5\%) and failed every image acceptance gate, Team~23 followed our POC failure-to-decision rule and scoped the December~2 demonstration to the workflow/uncertainty walkthrough, offline \texttt{experiment/} ML pilot scripts (acting as the POC CLI harness), synthetic boundary fixtures (\texttt{test/fixtures/generate.py}), and executable SRS/HA/design verification scripts, while staging the full FastAPI runtime and Chromium MV3 browser extension integration in \texttt{src/} for Revision~0 (matching Phase~2/3 staging in the SRS and Module Guide; \texttt{DevelopmentPlan.tex} will be updated accordingly alongside Revision~0).

\section{Team Charter Trigger Items}

\noindent \textbf{Trigger Summary:} Under Appendix~C of \href{https://github.com/richy1337/Verity/blob/main/pdfs/DevelopmentPlan/DevelopmentPlan.pdf}{DevelopmentPlan.pdf}, a formal Team Check-In is triggered when: (1) an issue is more than 7~days overdue with no status update, (2) a member misses two scheduled meetings in a 4-week window without prior notice, or (3) a member goes two consecutive weekly cycles with no substantive contribution.

\medskip\noindent \textbf{Observed Trigger Status:} No member missed scheduled meetings without notice or went inactive for two consecutive weeks. However, on an aggregate process level, the team violated our issue-currency expectation by leaving early September backlog issues (\href{https://github.com/richy1337/Verity/issues/4}{\#4}--\href{https://github.com/richy1337/Verity/issues/11}{\#11}) open well past 7~days after their underlying work landed in the SRS/Design docs, failing to log informal weekly syncs and lecture attendance as GitHub issues, and falling behind on documenting TA V\&V Plan feedback as tracked issues.

\medskip\noindent \textbf{Corrective Plan:} Ahead of the POC demo, we closed stale completed backlog issues with commit/PR references, opened eight single-owner issues (\href{https://github.com/richy1337/Verity/issues/72}{\#72}--\href{https://github.com/richy1337/Verity/issues/79}{\#79}) covering all unaddressed TA V\&V feedback and recent PR defects, and committed to logging weekly meetings, lecture attendance, and \texttt{Co-authored-by} trailers consistently. Current trigger thresholds remain appropriate and do not need loosening.
